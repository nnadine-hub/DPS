Amalilia
Nile Cruise

About project

A dialogue between form & function

A full vessel interior, delivered from naval joinery through to the tableware. The brief was a boat that felt like a house on the river rather than a hotel that happened to float, so every room was planned around the view first and the circulation second.

Marquetry panels were cut in a Cairo workshop from the same timber as the stair, patterned rugs were woven to the plan of each floor, and the bar was tiled in a green glaze mixed to match the water at four in the afternoon.

We bring together architecture, design, art, craft, and culture — local makers, international suppliers, galleries we’ve worked with for decades — and we arrange them around a single question: what does this particular client, in this particular place, actually need?
