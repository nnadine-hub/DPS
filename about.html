About us

We are designers, architects, curators, — bringing together art, precision, and culture into spaces that feel inevitable. Cairo‑rooted. Context-driven.

See projects

Our story

Spaces make peace Peace makes life

We are a boutique design studio that specializes in high-end residential and commercial and landscape projects, alongside installations, event design, and product design. The studio’s portfolio extends across hospitality, retail, and F&B, and its network spans regional and international makers and manufacturers.

Work begins with the site and the client rather than a house style. What survives from the building, what can be made locally, and what has to be brought in — those three answers shape everything that follows.

Design Point
Studio ethos

We bring together architecture, design, art, craft, and culture — local makers, international suppliers, galleries we’ve worked with for decades — and we arrange them around a single question: what does this particular client, in this particular place, actually need? We build each project around a concept rooted in the client’s persona or brand. The spaces we love most are the ones that feel like home.

We stay curious by staying close. Our founders and team are constantly immersed — involved in cultural events, attentive to human nature, and constantly in motion. Egypt is our muse. Its textures, its history, its contradictions are always rooted in our process. Every detail is considered, every decision deliberate.

Nothing is built from scratch. Every project is a conversation between what exists and what is possible. Twenty-three years in, we continue to focus on projects that intrigue us, allowing us to create cross-cultural dialogue through the spaces we design.

Core values

Creating timeless interiors inspired by culture and craft

  1. 01

    Authenticity

    Every design reflects the identity, character, and aspirations of its people. We create spaces that feel genuine to those who inhabit them, translating personal stories and cultural context into thoughtful environments.

  2. 02

    Purpose

    We believe every space should serve a purpose beyond its appearance. Through thoughtful planning and intentional design, we balance beauty and functionality to create environments that enrich everyday living.

  3. 03

    Craftsmanship

    Through careful material selection, refined techniques, and a dedication to quality, we bring every vision to life, creating spaces that reflect precision, authenticity, and a lasting appreciation for the art of making.

  4. 04

    Respect for
    heritage

    We draw inspiration from the stories, traditions, and cultural identity that shape the spaces around us. We create meaningful environments that preserve their roots and carry their essence into the future.

Experience

2022

Two full vessel interiors handed over in the same season. The studio ran the joinery packages with yards in Alexandria and Cairo, and specified every soft furnishing, rug and service piece down to the glassware.

2023

A ground-floor workshop next to the office gave us space to prototype in-house. That year we started the Madar restoration, stripping decades of overpaint from original panelling and matching new plasterwork to what survived.

2024

A festival build measured in days rather than months. The arches were fabricated flat, trucked to the coast and raised on site, lit so the entrance would photograph cleanly from any angle on the carpet.

2025

Two residential handovers and a first move into landscape, working with a Cairo nursery on planting that would survive the courtyard microclimate rather than fight it.
